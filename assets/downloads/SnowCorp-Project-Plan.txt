SECURITY INVESTIGATION
Case reference: SC-SEC-001
Report: 01-Failed-Logon-and-Lockout-Investigation.txt
Location: 07-Security-Investigation

Reviewed the earlier controlled lockout on October 19, 2026:
DC01 event 4740: policy.test locked out; caller CLIENT01.
DC01 event 4767: SNOWCORP\Administrator unlocked policy.test.

The recorded events were 5 minutes and 6 seconds apart.

Performed a fresh controlled incorrect-password test
on October 25, 2026 and correlated:

DC01 event 4771:
Kerberos pre-authentication failure.
Failure code: 0x18.
Client address: ::ffff:10.10.10.20, matching CLIENT01.

CLIENT01 event 4625:
Failed interactive sign-in for policy.test.
Logon type: 2.
Status: 0xC000006D.
Substatus: 0xC000006A, indicating an incorrect password.

Correlated the account, timestamps, and source information.

The CLIENT01 event's 127.0.0.1 address is loopback and was
not treated as the address of a remote attacker.

The October 19 lockout and October 25 failed sign-in are
separate controlled tests, not one continuous incident.
