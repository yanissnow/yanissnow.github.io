REPORTED PROBLEM
The temporary domain account policy.test could not sign in on CLIENT01
after deliberately incorrect passwords were submitted during the
account-lockout policy test.

IMPACT AND SCOPE
The affected account was the dedicated Policy Test account.
The test was performed on the isolated SnowCorp lab network.
The account had no assigned department group membership.

ENVIRONMENT
Domain: snowcorp.test
NetBIOS name: SNOWCORP
Domain controller: DC01
Workstation: CLIENT01
Account: SNOWCORP\policy.test
User principal name: policy.test@snowcorp.test
Account location: SnowCorp/Employees/IT

RELEVANT POLICY
Policy: Default Domain Policy
Account lockout threshold: 10 failed attempts
Account lockout duration: 15 minutes
Reset account lockout counter after: 15 minutes

The effective domain policy was verified during Session 7.
The exact number of incorrect submissions in the test was not
independently counted.

INITIAL EVIDENCE
CLIENT01 first displayed an invalid-credentials delay message.
Testing continued until it explicitly reported that the referenced
account was currently locked out and could not be logged on.

On DC01, the account's Account tab in Active Directory Users and
Computers separately showed that the account was currently locked out.

TROUBLESHOOTING
1. Used the dedicated policy.test account for the controlled test.
2. Established a successful initial sign-in on CLIENT01.
3. Submitted deliberately incorrect passwords.
4. Distinguished the initial sign-in delay from the later explicit
   account-lockout message.
5. Checked the matching account in Active Directory on DC01 and
   confirmed its locked-out state.
6. Unlocked the account as an administrator.
7. Signed in on CLIENT01 and checked the active account with whoami.
8. Signed out and disabled the temporary test account after testing.

CAUSE ASSESSMENT
The controlled incorrect-password submissions triggered the configured
domain account-lockout protection. The resulting sign-in denial was
the intended security-policy behavior.

The initial delay message alone was insufficient to establish a
domain account lockout. The explicit workstation message and the
separate Active Directory check established the account state.

RESOLUTION
An administrator unlocked policy.test on DC01.
The existing account-lockout policy was retained.
Successful sign-in was then verified on CLIENT01.

VERIFICATION
CLIENT01 displayed the account's desktop after the unlock.
The whoami command returned:
snowcorp\policy.test

This confirmed successful sign-in as the intended domain account.
